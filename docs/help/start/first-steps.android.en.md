Tessera is a task tracker: boards with tasks, documents, notes and reminders. This article walks the path from an empty account to your first task on a board — the way it looks in the Android app.

## Workspace

Everything lives inside a **workspace**. A workspace is a self-contained space with its own projects, boards and members. Your first workspace is created together with your account, so you never start from nothing.

The app's navigation is tucked into the **side menu**: the “☰” button at the left of the top bar, or a swipe from the left edge of the screen. At the top of the menu is the current workspace; tapping it switches to another, and the “＋” button next to it creates a new one.

To invite a colleague: the workspace menu (“…” next to its name) → “Members” → “Invite by email”. The invitee gets an email with a link; the role is “Member” or “Admin”, and the workspace has a single owner whose role is never changed.

## Projects and groups

The project tree is in the same side menu, below the sections. Projects are filed into **groups**, and groups nest inside one another. The “＋” button in the “PROJECTS” heading creates a project or a group at the root; every node in the tree has the same button. To move a project into a group, **press and hold** its row and drag — the tree scrolls on its own as you carry the project to the edge of the screen.

Tapping a project opens its board and closes the menu.

## Board

A board belongs to a project and is made of columns. A new board arrives with four columns already — you can rename them, add your own and reorder them.

On a phone the columns do not fit across the screen, so the board **swipes sideways**: one column takes up almost the full width, and its neighbours peek in from the edge. Empty columns can be collapsed into narrow strips, so the board scrolls through faster.

A task is created with the field **at the end of a column**: scroll the list to the bottom and type a title. To move a card to another column, **press and hold** it and drag — or open the task and change its column with the status chip, which is usually faster on a phone.

Tapping a card opens the **task screen**: description, subtasks, tags, assignees, priority, due date and comments.

One of the board's columns is the **completing** one: a task that lands in it counts as done and is stamped with a completion date. What matters is landing in the column, not the task's title — see [Boards and tasks](/help/boards-and-tasks) for more.
