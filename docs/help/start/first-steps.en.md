Tessera is a task tracker: boards with tasks, documents, notes and reminders. This article walks the path from an empty account to your first task on a board.

## Workspace

Everything lives inside a **workspace**. A workspace is a self-contained space with its own projects, boards and members; you switch between them in the header of the sidebar. Your first workspace is created together with your account, so you never start from nothing.

To invite a colleague: the workspace menu (its name at the top of the sidebar) → **Members**. Addresses in the invite field turn into chips — on <kbd>Enter</kbd>, a comma or a space — so several people can be invited at once; **Invite** sends. A registered person is added at once, a new one gets an email with a link (the link can be copied too). The role is “Member” or “Admin”; the workspace has a single owner whose role is never changed.

## Projects and groups

On the left is the project tree. Projects can be filed into **groups**, and groups nest inside one another: handy when you have many directions of work. The “+” button in the “Projects” heading creates a project or a group at the root; every node in the tree has the same button. Drag a project into a group with the mouse.

Above the tree is **Favourites**: the boards you starred next to the board breadcrumbs (or with <kbd>⇧F</kbd>). It shows up once it holds at least one board, and everyone has their own. Drag rows to reorder them; “Remove from favourites” is in a row’s menu (“⋯” or right-click), and “Undo” in the toast brings a removed board back. The arrow keys and <kbd>G</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> walk through Favourites just like the tree; in the <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> palette favourite boards come as a group of their own and first among search results.

## Board

A board belongs to a project and is made of columns. A new board arrives with four columns already — you can rename them, add your own and reorder them.

A task is created with the “+” button in a column header and moved between columns by dragging. Clicking a card opens the task window: description, subtasks, tags, assignees, priority, due date and comments.

One of the board's columns is the **completing** one: a task that lands in it counts as done and is stamped with a completion date. What matters is landing in the column, not the task's title or a checkbox — see [Boards and tasks](/help/boards-and-tasks) for more.

At the bottom of the space rail (the narrow strip on the left) there is a button with a question mark, and inside it two items. **Get started** is a tour of the interface right inside the app: chapters, a checklist on «My tasks» and hints at new elements — see [Tour and hints](/help/hints); you can launch it at any time and go through it again. **Help centre** is this help: it opens as a window over your current screen, so the board you called it from never goes away. Faster still is <kbd>F1</kbd>: it opens the help right at the article about the current screen, and it closes it too.

In the help window the sections and articles are on the left, the search is at the top (<kbd>/</kbd> puts the cursor there, <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> and <kbd>↵</kbd> pick a result), and the article's contents are on the right. At the bottom of an article you can say **whether it helped**: one answer per article, and administrators see the totals under Administration → Help, which tells them which articles need rewriting.
