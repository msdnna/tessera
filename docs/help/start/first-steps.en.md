Tessera is a task tracker: boards with tasks, documents, notes and reminders. This article walks the path from an empty account to your first task on a board.

## Workspace

Everything lives inside a **workspace**. A workspace is a self-contained space with its own projects, boards and members; you switch between them in the header of the sidebar. Your first workspace is created together with your account, so you never start from nothing.

To invite a colleague: the **Members** button (the people icon) in the sidebar header, to the right of the logo → “Invite by email”. The invitee gets an email with a link; the role is “Member” or “Admin”, and the workspace has a single owner whose role is never changed.

## Projects and groups

On the left is the project tree. Projects can be filed into **groups**, and groups nest inside one another: handy when you have many directions of work. The “+” button in the “Projects” heading creates a project or a group at the root; every node in the tree has the same button. Drag a project into a group with the mouse.

## Board

A board belongs to a project and is made of columns. A new board arrives with four columns already — you can rename them, add your own and reorder them.

A task is created with the “+” button in a column header and moved between columns by dragging. Clicking a card opens the task window: description, subtasks, tags, assignees, priority, due date and comments.

One of the board's columns is the **completing** one: a task that lands in it counts as done and is stamped with a completion date. What matters is landing in the column, not the task's title or a checkbox — see [Boards and tasks](/help/boards-and-tasks) for more.
