In the browser the task window has a **Documents** tab that answers one question: which documents talk about this task — a spec, a meeting record, a policy.

**There is no such tab in the app.** The task window has six tabs — «Description», «Comments», «Subtasks», «Relations», «Files», «History» — and document links are not among them. The [document reader](/help/documents) doesn't show them either: there is no «Links and approvals» panel on a phone.

So the short answer is: **a task's links to documents live in the web version only** — they are created there and viewed there. What follows explains what those links are, so that meeting them in the browser doesn't mean starting from scratch.

## Not to be confused with «Relations» and «Files»

Two tabs in the app sound similar but are something else:

- **«Relations»** — the task's links **to other tasks** (blocks, duplicates, relates to). Documents never appear there. See [Task relations](/help/task-links).
- **«Files»** — the task's own attachments: images, `pdf`s, archives. Those are files, not Tessera documents.

A Tessera document is an entry in the [Documents](/help/documents) section, with its tree, versions and approvals.

## The link is made from the document's side

Even in the browser, the Documents tab has **no "add" button**. The link is created in the document itself — in the «Links and approvals» panel (the chain glyph in the document's header), with the «Link a task» button.

That is deliberate. A link makes sense where the context is: you are reading the paragraph about data import and attach the import task next to it. From the task window there is no way to see which part of the document it refers to — and that is the most valuable thing about such a link. It is also why the link is awkward to make from a phone: you have to select a paragraph first.

## A link to the whole document or to one block

In a document a link can be made in two ways:

- **To the whole document** — when nothing is selected. The usual case: "this task is about this policy".
- **To a specific block** — when a paragraph, list item or heading is selected. Such a link answers "which clause exactly is the task about".

A block link shows a **grey quote** next to the document's title — a short fragment of that very paragraph. The quote is captured **when the link is created** and never changes afterwards: the paragraph gets edited later, and the saved quote is the only thing that will explain what was meant six months on. If the fragment was rewritten entirely, the link is worth remaking.

## What to do from a phone

Reading a document works here: the «Documents» item in the side menu opens the tree and the reader. What the app won't give you is the hop from a task to its document and back — the link between them isn't shown on a phone, and you'll have to find the document by its title.

If a task grew out of a document and that matters on the phone, mention the document **in the task's description** as plain text. The description is always visible in the app, unlike the link.
