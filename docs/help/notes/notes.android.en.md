A note is a short record that doesn't need a task: the outcome of a call, a list of questions, a draft of some wording. The section opens from the **“Notes”** item in the side menu (the “☰” on the left of the top bar).

![The Notes section in the app](../assets/notes-mobile-light.png)

## How it works

First comes the list of the workspace's notes. A tap on a note slides an **editor out over the list**: a title and text. The **“New note”** button creates an empty one and opens it straight away.

Changes are recorded with the **“Save”** button: go back without it and your edits won't be saved. Deletion is confirmed separately so you don't lose a record with a stray tap.

The text is plain Markdown, the same as on the web: a note started on a phone opens on a computer as-is.

Notes belong to a workspace: switch the workspace in the side-menu header and you see its notes.

## A note or a document

The rule is simple: a note is for yourself and off the cuff, a [document](/help/documents) is for working together. On a phone the difference is even sharper: here notes can be written and edited, while documents are read-only.

## Search

Notes take part in the app's global search — the same place as tasks, documents and help articles. Search by a word from the title or the text; there's no need to enter the section for it.
