**Delete** on a task no longer erases it at once. The task goes to the **board's trash** and stays there for **30 days** with its whole history, comments, attachments and subtasks. After 30 days the trash deletes it for good.

## Delete and change your mind

**Delete** is in the card menu (the three dots in the card's corner) and in the action row at the bottom of the task window. It asks no confirmation: the task leaves the board at once, and a **Task #N moved to the trash** message appears at the bottom of the screen for a few seconds with a **Restore** button. Pressed it by mistake — tap Restore, and the task is back with its subtasks.

## The whole trash is in the web version

The list of a board's deleted tasks with Restore and Delete forever is in the web version for now: the board's “⋯” menu → **Trash**. It also shows who deleted a task, when, and how many days it has left.

A deleted task disappears everywhere — from the board, My tasks, search and links — and no reminders arrive for it.

## Delete forever in the archive

In an archived card's menu, **Delete forever** erases the task at once, past the trash: it sits behind a confirmation naming the task. The [archive](/help/archive) is for finished work, the trash is for what was deleted.
