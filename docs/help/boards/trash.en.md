**Delete** on a task no longer erases it at once. The task goes to the **board's trash** and stays there for **30 days**: all that time it can be brought back with its whole history, comments, attachments and subtasks. After 30 days the trash deletes it for good.

## Delete and change your mind

**Delete** is in the card menu (right-click or “⋯” on the card), on a list and calendar row and in the task window's “⋯” menu. It asks no confirmation: the task leaves the board at once, and a **Task #N moved to the trash** message appears at the bottom with an **Undo** button (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Z</kbd>). Pressed it by mistake — undo, and the task is back where it was.

Subtasks go to the trash **together with their parent** and come back with it. A subtask deleted on its own earlier stays in the trash as a row of its own — bringing the parent back leaves it there.

## Where the trash is

The trash opens from the board menu — the **“⋯”** button on the right of the board bar, the **Trash** entry. It shows up as a panel on the right of the board (a sheet from the bottom on a phone) and lists the tasks deleted from this board in the last 30 days, newest first. Each row has:

- the task's number and title;
- who deleted it, when, and **how many days** it has left;
- how many subtasks went with it;
- **Restore** — the task is back on the board at once, in its column;
- **Delete forever** — in two presses: the first arms the button, the second erases the task with its files. That one can't be undone.

If you restore a subtask while its parent is still in the trash, it comes back as a card of its own at the end of its column.

## The “about me” message

When a colleague deletes a task you took part in, a “deleted the task” notification arrives. On the pop-up, **To trash** opens the board with the trash and lights the task up — one press brings it back.

## What the trash leaves out

A deleted task disappears **everywhere**: from the board, My tasks, search and the palette, links, stages and counters. No reminders or due-date notifications arrive for it. Its GitLab link is kept, but the sync neither touches the task nor creates it anew while it is in the trash.

## Trash and archive are different things

- The [**archive**](/help/archive) is for finished work worth keeping: a task stays there as long as you like and is view-only.
- The **trash** is for what was deleted: after 30 days the task goes by itself.

Deleting a board or a column is reversible too, but briefly: right after the delete there is a message with an undo, and they are deleted for good a day later. The tasks of a deleted column don't vanish — they move to the neighbouring column.
