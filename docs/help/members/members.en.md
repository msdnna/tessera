The **Members** window opens from the workspace menu (its name at the top of the sidebar). On the left are four sections: **Members**, **Invitations**, **Groups** and **Roles and permissions**; digits `1`–`4` switch them, `Esc` closes the window.

## Roles

A workspace has three roles:

- **Owner** — one per workspace. Can do everything, including handing the workspace over and deleting it.
- **Admin** — invites and removes members, changes roles (except the owner's), decides access requests, makes groups and changes the workspace settings.
- **Member** — works with tasks, boards, documents, notes and meetings.

**Roles and permissions** shows this as a table. A guest role limited to chosen projects and custom roles are still in the works — they are marked "Soon".

## Who was here when

On the right of a member's row are their groups and when they were last active: **now**, **12 min ago**, **yesterday**. A green dot means the person was in the app within the last quarter of an hour. The tooltip tells "in the app" apart from "last action here" — the latter counts only changes in this workspace; just looking at a board does not move it.

## Hand over ownership

The owner opens another member's role menu and picks **Owner — hand over ownership…**. A form appears under the row: type the workspace name and **Hand over ownership** turns on. The new owner is notified, and you stay in the workspace as an admin. Only the new owner can hand it back.

## Leave the workspace

On your own row press **⋯** → **Leave the workspace** and confirm with a second press. The owner can't leave — they hand the workspace over first, or it would be left without one.

## Groups

A group names several members at once: "Frontend", "Backend", "Design". In **Groups** the owner or an admin types a name and presses `↵` (or `N`), then ticks the people in it. Click a name to rename it; colour and deletion are in the **⋯** menu, and **Undo** in the toast brings a deleted group back.

A group shows up in a task's assignee picker — one row assigns everyone in it, picking it again takes them all off — and in the editor's `@` popup, where picking a group writes a mention of each member. A group grants no rights: it is the people who are assigned and mentioned.

## Access requests

Under the invite block there is the **Workspace link**. Whoever opens it (or a board's link) without being a member sees a **No access** screen with **Ask for access**, and may add a few words. The screen names neither the workspace nor its owner — the person learns those once they are let in.

The owner and admins are notified, and the request appears in the Members window under **Asking for access**: **Let in** adds the person as a member (the role can be changed later), **Decline** closes the request. The author hears back in their notifications; after a refusal they can ask again in a day.
