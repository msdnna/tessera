## How do I turn on the dark theme?

The side menu (“☰” at the left of the top bar) → the gear at the bottom → **“Theme”**: “System”, “Light” or “Dark”. System follows your phone's setting. The same place holds the interface accent colour, the date and time format and the first day of the week.

## A task is in the last column but isn't counted as done

A task is made done by the board's **completing column**, and that column is set explicitly in the board settings (by default it's the rightmost one). If columns have been reordered or a new one was added on the right, check that the column you have in mind is the one marked as completing; you can assign a different one from the web version. See [Boards and tasks](/help/boards-and-tasks) for more.

## How do I invite someone into a workspace?

The workspace menu (“…” next to its name in the side menu header) → “Members” → “Invite by email”. The invitation arrives as an email with a link; the role is “Member” or “Admin”. You change the role of an existing member in the same window.

## Why can't documents be edited from the phone?

That's by design: in the app the **Documents** section is read-only. The block editor with paragraph comments and conflict resolution is a project of its own, and hastily ported to a phone it would lose text more often than it helped. See [Documents](/help/documents) for more.

## Reminders aren't arriving

Check two system permissions: notifications (Android 13 and later asks for them separately) and background activity — an app that has “gone to sleep” to save battery may fire the signal later than scheduled. See [Reminders](/help/reminders) for more.

## Where do I see the app version?

At the very bottom of the side menu, under your name: `v<version>` — the app, `API <version>` — the server, whenever it last replied. A mismatch is visible at a glance.

## The app said a new version is out

The app checks on its own whether a new release is out and offers to download and install it. Accept when it suits you: your data lives on the server, and nothing is lost during an update. After an update the **“What's new”** window is shown once, with the highlights of the releases you missed.

## Does the app work without the internet?

Partly. Boards, tasks, documents and notes live on the server, so without a connection they won't open. **Help — will open:** the articles live inside the app, and searching them works offline too.

## I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen there's a “Forgot password” link: an email with a reset link will arrive. If the email doesn't come, check your spam folder.
