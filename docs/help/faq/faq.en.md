## How do I turn on the dark theme?

**Settings** → theme: “System”, “Light” or “Dark”. System follows your operating system's setting. The same place lets you pick the interface accent colour, the date and time format and the first day of the week.

## A task is in the last column but isn't counted as done

A task is made done by the board's **completing column**, and that column is set explicitly in the board settings (by default it's the rightmost one). If columns have been reordered or a new one was added on the right, check that the column you have in mind is the one marked as completing. See [Boards and tasks](/help/boards-and-tasks) for more.

## How do I invite someone into a workspace?

The **Members** button (the people icon) in the sidebar header, to the right of the logo → “Invite by email”. The invitation arrives as an email with a link; the role is “Member” or “Admin”. You change the role of an existing member in the same window.

## Is there a mobile app?

Yes, there's an Android client — the install link is in the app, and there's a download button in the interface. The app works online and can send push notifications for reminders; the web version doesn't send push.

## What is the “Notes” section and how does it differ from documents?

**Notes** are quick jottings for yourself: open, write, close. **Documents** are for collaboration, with formatting, version history and comments. If a note is meant for colleagues or will outlive a week — it belongs in documents.

## Where did the “What's new” window go?

It's shown once after an update. You can open it again from the same place where you saw the version number — the client and server versions are shown in the bottom-right corner of the sidebar, and hovering shows the commit and the build date.

## I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen there's a “Forgot password” link: an email with a reset link will arrive. If the email doesn't come, check your spam folder.

## The app said a new version is out

That's normal: the web client notices a new release on its own and offers to reload. Nothing gets lost — accept when it suits you.
